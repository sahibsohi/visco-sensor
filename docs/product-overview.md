# Product overview

## Problem

Visco-Sensor explores whether accessible longitudinal monitoring could help patients and healthcare professionals recognize changes sooner and communicate more effectively.

## Intended experience

The software platform is planned around three users:

- **Patient:** views understandable trends, device status, and educational wellness prompts.
- **Clinician:** reviews synthetic trend summaries and acknowledges demonstration alerts.
- **Developer:** can run reproducible device scenarios and inspect the complete data path.

## Research inputs

Two independent qualitative interview efforts shaped the concept:

- 55 older adults and family members discussed accessibility, independence, and caregiver communication.
- More than 150 patients discussed monitoring and preventative-care needs.

These interviews informed product direction. They do not constitute clinical validation or a controlled study.

## Day 1 outcome

The first milestone provides the engineering foundation: clear service ownership, runnable health endpoints, development infrastructure, quality tooling, and safety boundaries.
